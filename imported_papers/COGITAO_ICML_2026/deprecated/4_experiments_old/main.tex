\section{Experiments}

To showcase the utility of our Before-ARC generator, we designed a set of experiments to demonstrate how our generator can be leveraged to study visual compositionality and generalization. In designing these experiments, we were inspired by various NLP works which enable substantial control over the level of compositionality and generalization \cite{SCAN_lake_baroni, keysers2019measuring,kim2020cogs}. To further demonstrate the superiority of our generator compared to other works in the visual domain, we attempted to replicate our suggested experiment settings on two analogous visual reasoning datasets: CVR \cite{cvr2022} and RE-ARC \cite{reverse_engineer_hodel} - which results we discuss in the main manuscript (but report the details of in the appendix). We remind the reader that our generator is not limited to these experiments and can be repurposed to design different experiments - we thus encourage the community to explore other configurations of Before-ARC.
