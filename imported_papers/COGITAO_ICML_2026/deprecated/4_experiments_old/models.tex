\subsection{Models} \label{models}

We aimed to compare the frequently used architecture of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) and Visual Transformers (ViT) and evaluate the compositionality and generalization abilities of these models. We were particularly interested in identifying the shortcomings of these models, and how they could be improved. Recent research \cite{arc_vit} has enhanced ViT performance on object-centric visual reasoning tasks by optimizing object and positional encoding - we also replicate some of these experiments.
Finally, we attempted to use two novel algorithms which have shown promise in the field of visual reasoning: Diffusion Transformer \cite{diffusion_transformer} and Looped Transformer \cite{looped_transformer}.
