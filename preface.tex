The journey culminating in this thesis, \emph{\thesistitle}, has been anything but a straight line. If my publication record leading up to this point appears somewhat fragmented, it is a direct reflection of my genuine, unbridled excitement for the field of Artificial Intelligence. I have always been drawn to diverse ideas and collaborations, and while this led me down varied research paths, at the given time and context, pursuing every single one of those threads made perfect sense. Ultimately, it was this broad curiosity that brought the two sides of this thesis together. Neuroscience has long argued about how a computation should be spread across the neurons that carry it, and the chapters that follow measure what a training procedure decides about that.

This journey truly began when my original plan to pursue a full-time Master's degree was wonderfully derailed. I stayed on through my subsequent studies and into this PhD, always feeling incredibly fortunate for the immense freedom and decision-making power I was given to explore the topics I was most passionate about.
