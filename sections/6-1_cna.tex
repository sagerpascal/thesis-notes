\section{Chapter Introduction}

Every chapter so far has taken a property that training leaves open and asked
what settles it. The learning rule settles how much of a network carries the
computation, the training objective settles the symmetry of a weight matrix,
and the norm of a predicted latent is settled by nothing at all until a probe
is asked to read it. In each case the property is a consequence of how the
network was trained, and in each case it could have come out otherwise.

This chapter asks what it would mean to stop leaving the property to training.
In the Cooperative Network Architecture a sensory pattern is a set of neurons
that hold one another in place, so redundancy of support belongs to the
architecture rather than something a
regulariser has to be coaxed into producing. The parts are learned, by Hebbian
plasticity from data, and how they are joined is not.

The relation to \cref{chap:grokking} is a convergence and not a consequence,
and the dates on the two publication boxes say why. The manuscript below went out in $2024$,
two years ahead of the grokking study, so no argument can run from that study
to this architecture. What is true is that an architecture designed for its own
reasons arrives at the same mechanism, redundancy of support, that a credit
assignment rule can be made to produce by training. The chapter is offered as
a proposal, and \cref{chap:discussion} says what would have to be measured
before it is more than that.

A note on vocabulary. The paper's \emph{net} is a representation of one
sensory pattern, a set of neurons bound together by recurrent connections, and
a \emph{net fragment} is a reusable piece of one. Neither is the thesis's
circuit, which is a count of participating units in a trained feedforward
layer. This chapter computes no participation ratio and runs no deletion
protocol, so the connection to the earlier chapters is through the mechanism
and not through a shared measurement.

\section{Chapter Summary}
% The chapter drops the paper's own discussion and this section replaces it.
% The paper's body says "as we discuss in Section~\ref{sec:discussion}", so
% that label belongs here now. If this section stops being the replacement for
% the paper's discussion, move the label or reword the reference.
\importedlabel{cna}{sec:discussion}

A net represents a sensory pattern as a set of neurons bound to one another by
recurrent connections, assembled from fragments that Hebbian plasticity learns
from data. Because a neuron belongs to the representation only while other
neurons hold it there, support is structural, and three consequences follow
that the chapter demonstrates.

Noise is filtered without being modelled. A spurious activation has nothing
supporting it, so it fails to join a net, and the pattern remains
distinguishable with up to about $59\%$ of the input corrupted. Occluded
figures are completed, because the fragments that are present support the
parts that are missing. And fragments learned separately recombine: a network
whose fragments were learned on straight lines assembles representations of
digits and characters it was never trained on, which is compositional
generalisation obtained from the structure of the representation rather than
from more data.

The features the model composes are hand designed rather than learned end to
end. The demonstrations
are on line drawings, and the architecture does not scale to natural images.
And no dynamics are defined over a net, so there is nothing in the
representation to plan with. These are the three limits that
\cref{sec:bg_substratelimits} identifies in the whole lineage this work
belongs to, and this work inherits all three.

\Cref{chap:discussion} brings the four results together, states what the
thesis has shown about what a task leaves open, applies the standard of
\cref{chap:bounded} to the measure of \cref{chap:grokking}, and says what
would have to be built and measured for the proposal in this chapter to stand
on more than a demonstration.
